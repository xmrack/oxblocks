Outputs the leaves

Each leaf is an output's one-time key and amount commitment, as they are on the chain. The tree turns them into six numbers: the coordinates of the key, of the point its key image is made from, and of the commitment. For an output from before Carrot it first clears any torsion from the key and commitment, and hashes the key to that point the older way; that is the legacy kind. A group of 38 hashes to one node of layer 1.
