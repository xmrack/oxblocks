The climb, group by group

What a wallet holds to prove {% if outputs_total == 1 %}its output is{% else %}these outputs are{% endif %} in the tree, from the leaves up. Each group hashes to one member of the group above it, and the root at the top is the one the block records. The members on the path are marked, with their values in full.

    Each leaf is an output's one-time key and amount commitment, as they are on the chain. The tree turns them into six numbers: the coordinates of the key, of the point its key image is made from, and of the commitment. For an output from before Carrot it first clears any torsion from the key and commitment, and hashes the key to that point the older way; that is the legacy kind.
